  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifier, language, time zone.
  • Game usage data: matches played, performance statistics, acquired skins, progression, session time.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see our Cookies Policy.
  • Advertising data: when you view ads served by Google AdSense or other ad networks.

If you choose to log in via third-party services (Google, Discord, etc.), we receive the basic data those services share with us according to their own policies.

3. Minors

The Service is intended for persons over 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete such information immediately.

If you are under 18, you must have authorization from your parents or legal guardians to use the Service.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data without your authorization, contact us at legal@bunkertank.io.

6. Your Rights

6.1 Rights Under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012

As a personal data subject in Colombia, you have the right to:

  • Know, update, and rectify your personal data.
  • Request proof of the authorization granted to the Controller.
  • Be informed about the use of your data.
  • File complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) for legal violations.
  • Revoke authorization and/or request deletion of your data when constitutional and legal principles, rights, and guarantees are not respected.
  • Free access to your personal data.

6.2 Rights Under GDPR (EU)

If you reside in the European Union, you additionally have the right to:

  • Portability of your data in a structured format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Restrict processing.
  • Automated decisions: right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

6.3 Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California)

If you reside in California, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

VIINZO STUDIOS expressly declares that it does NOT sell personal information of its users.

6.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, send a request to legal@bunkertank.io indicating:

  1. Full name and ID document.
  2. Email associated with your Bunkertank account.
  3. Right you wish to exercise and detailed description.

We will respond within a maximum of 15 business days (Colombia) or 30 calendar days (GDPR/CCPA), as applicable.
