Refund Policy — Bunkertank

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Refund Policy governs the conditions under which VIINZO STUDIOS S.A.S. (Tax ID/NIT 901.787.694-7), operator of the web game Bunkertank (https://bunkertank.io), processes refund requests for Virtual Content and other digital products purchased through the Service.

This policy complies with the Colombian Consumer Statute (Law 1480 of 2011), the EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, and other applicable regulations in jurisdictions where the Service operates.

1. Nature of Digital Products

Bunkertank exclusively sells non-tangible digital goods including:

  • Premium currency ("gems")
  • Skins (cosmetic appearances for tanks)
  • In-game power-ups and upgrades
  • Season passes and subscriptions, if applicable

These products are delivered instantly to the User's account upon payment confirmation by Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor.

2. General Refund Policy

2.1 Consumed or Used Digital Goods

No refunds are granted for digital goods that have already been:

  • Partially or fully consumed within the game (spent gems, activated power-ups).
  • Applied to a skin or customization already equipped.
  • Used to unlock content or features.

This exception is based on Article 47 of Law 1480/2011 (Colombia) and Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU, which exclude from the right of withdrawal digital content supplied immediately when the consumer has expressly consented to the loss of the right of withdrawal.

2.2 Unused Digital Goods

The following cases will be considered for refund, within 14 calendar days following the purchase:

Case Eligibility
Accidental unused purchase (unspent gems, unequipped skin) Eligible
Duplicate purchase due to technical error Eligible
Product not delivered after confirmed payment Eligible
Unauthorized or fraudulent charge Eligible
Minor user without parental authorization Eligible
Service failure preventing enjoyment of purchase Eligible

2.3 Right of Withdrawal (EU and Colombia consumers)

Consumers residing in the European Union or Colombia have the right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 calendar days after the transaction, provided that the digital content acquired has not been used.

To exercise this right, send an email to soporte@bunkertank.io with:

  1. Full name and account email.
  2. Transaction identifier (Lemon Squeezy receipt).
  3. Express statement of withdrawal.

3. Non-Eligible Refunds

VIINZO STUDIOS will NOT issue refunds in the following cases:

  1. When more than 14 days have elapsed since the purchase.
  2. When the Virtual Content has already been consumed or used.
  3. When the request responds to dissatisfaction with the game's mechanics, balance, difficulty, or design changes.
  4. When the User has been suspended or canceled for violation of the Terms of Service (cheats, fraud, abusive behavior).
  5. When refund is requested for bonuses granted for free (rewarded ads, daily rewards, events).
  6. In case of fraudulent chargebacks (disputes initiated without legitimate justification before the issuing bank), VIINZO STUDIOS reserves the right to:
    • Permanently cancel the account.
    • Report the User to anti-fraud networks.
    • Initiate legal action for breach of contract.

4. Procedure to Request a Refund

Step 1: Contact Support

Send an email to soporte@bunkertank.io with subject "Refund Request – [Transaction ID]", including:

  • Full name of the account holder.
  • Email associated with the Bunkertank account.
  • Transaction identifier (Lemon Squeezy receipt).
  • Date and amount of the purchase.
  • Detailed reason for the request.
  • Screenshots or evidence, if applicable.

Step 2: Evaluation

VIINZO STUDIOS will evaluate the request within 5 business days. We may request additional information to validate eligibility.

Step 3: Resolution

If the request is eligible:

  • The refund will be made to the same payment method used in the original purchase.
  • Processing may take between 5 and 15 business days, depending on the bank or payment provider.
  • Lemon Squeezy will notify the User by email.
  • The refunded Virtual Content will be removed from the User's account.

If the request is rejected, the User will be notified explaining the reasons for the rejection.

5. Disputes with the Payment Processor

If the User initiates a dispute or chargeback directly with their bank or card issuer without first contacting soporte@bunkertank.io, VIINZO STUDIOS reserves the right to:

  • Suspend the User's account until the dispute is resolved.
  • Provide evidence to Lemon Squeezy and the issuing bank.
  • Permanently cancel the account if the dispute is declared fraudulent.

We always recommend contacting support first to resolve the situation amicably.

6. Refunds After Service Closure

If VIINZO STUDIOS decides to permanently discontinue the Service:

  • Users will be notified with at least 90 days notice.
  • Purchases made in the 6 months prior to the closure announcement will be refundable proportionally.
  • Virtual Content not consumed at the time of closure may be subject to compensation, evaluated case by case.

7. Modifications to This Policy

VIINZO STUDIOS may modify this Refund Policy at any time. Material changes will be notified at least 30 days in advance via email or prominent notice in the Service.

Modifications will apply to transactions made after entry into force.

8. Supervisory Authority and Complaints

If you believe this Policy or its application violates your rights as a consumer, you may contact:

In Colombia

  • Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) – https://www.sic.gov.co
  • Consumer protection office in your city.

In the European Union

  • Online Dispute Resolution Platform (ODR) – https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr
  • National consumer protection authority of your country.

In USA

  • Federal Trade Commission (FTC) – https://reportfraud.ftc.gov
  • Attorney General of your state.
