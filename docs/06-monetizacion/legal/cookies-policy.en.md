## 1. What Are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files stored in your browser or device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or session state.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as:

- **Local Storage / Session Storage**: browser storage for client data.
- **IndexedDB**: local browser database.
- **Pixels and web beacons**: invisible tracking images.
- **Third-party SDKs**: scripts that collect behavior information.

For purposes of this policy, all these technologies are collectively referred to as "cookies".

## 3. Consent Management

### 3.1 Cookie Banner

Upon first entering the Service, we display a banner that allows:

- **Accept all cookies**.
- **Reject all non-essential cookies**.
- **Customize the selection** by category (functionality, analytics, advertising).

Your choice is recorded and can be modified at any time.

### 3.2 Modify Your Consent

You can review or modify your preferences at any time through:

- The **"Cookie Settings"** link located in the Service footer.
- Deleting cookies from your browser and re-entering the Service.

### 3.3 Implications of Rejecting Cookies

- **Necessary cookies**: if you block them, the Service may not function correctly (you will not be able to log in, play multiplayer matches, or complete purchases).
- **Functionality cookies**: you will lose customizations and must reconfigure them in each session.
- **Analytics and advertising cookies**: does not affect Service functionality. You will continue to see advertising, but less relevant.

## 5. How to Control Cookies from Your Browser

You can manage or delete cookies directly from your browser settings. Each browser handles cookies differently:

- **Google Chrome**: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
- **Mozilla Firefox**: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox
- **Safari**: https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac
- **Microsoft Edge**: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge

You can also use the **"Do Not Track"** or **"Global Privacy Control (GPC)"** option of your browser. We honor these signals when technically possible.
